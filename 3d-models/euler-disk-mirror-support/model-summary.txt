  EULER'S DISK MIRROR SUPPORT - 3D MODEL SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📐 DIMENSIONS:
   • Outer Diameter: 210 mm (21 cm)
   • Inner Diameter: 195 mm (19.5 cm) - fits 20cm mirror
   • Height: 18 mm (1.8 cm)
   • Wall Thickness: 5 mm
   • Base Thickness: 3 mm

🎯 PURPOSE:
   Support for concave 20cm mirror (Euler's Disk)
   Prevents breakage by providing stable base

📦 FILES CREATED:
   ✓ mirror-support.stl (191 KB, 401 facets)
   ✓ mirror-support.scad (OpenSCAD source, parametric)
   ✓ README.md (Full documentation)
   ✓ QUICK-START.md (Quick guide)

🖨️ PRINTING INFO:
   • Material: PLA or PETG
   • Layer Height: 0.2 mm
   • Infill: 20-30%
   • Print Time: ~3-5 hours
   • Filament: ~60-80g
   • Supports: NOT needed
   • Brim: Recommended (10mm)

🔧 FEATURES:
   • Concave top surface (matches mirror shape)
   • 8 radial reinforcement ribs
   • Flat, stable base
   • Conical outer wall for easy placement

📝 CUSTOMIZATION:
   Open mirror-support.scad in OpenSCAD to adjust:
   - mirror_diameter
   - edge_height
   - support_diameter
   - wall_thickness
   - concave_radius
